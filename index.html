صحیح احادیث — سکون بخش مناظر کے ساتھ

Morning Aesthetics is a YouTube Shorts channel that shares authentic hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari and other reliable collections, narrated in Urdu over calm nature scenes, each with a short reflection (سبق).

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@MorningAesthetics

Morning Aesthetics Uploader

The channel uses a small private tool, Morning Aesthetics Uploader, run only by the channel owner on their own computer. It turns hadith text into short narrated videos and uploads them to the channel owner's own YouTube channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert). It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not read, collect or store anyone else's data.
